Use case: four multiplier-orb symbols for a slot-machine mobile playable ad, to be cut apart.
Subject: four separate crystal glass spheres, each with a glowing swirl of electric lightning inside, a thin gold ring band around the lower third, a small blue-gold gem below and tiny golden feathered wings on both sides (wing span about 150% of the sphere width). One row left to right, the spheres identical in design and size but different in colour of the glow and glass: (1) emerald green; (2) electric blue; (3) royal purple; (4) fiery red. The CENTRE of each sphere is a calm, brighter, relatively empty glowing area where a number will be added later (no number now).
Composition: one horizontal row of exactly four items, each centred in its quarter of the canvas, very wide empty gaps (at least 6% of the canvas width) between the wing tips, nothing touching, generous margins, on a flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background.
Constraints: exactly four items, no text, no letters, no digits, no logos, no shadows on the background, no glow or halo outside each object's own outline (the glow stays inside the glass; crisp outer edges). Original designs.
