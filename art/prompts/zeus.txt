Use case: the hero character sprite of a mobile slot-game ad, cut out on a flat key background so it can be placed over a sky and partly hidden behind a slot frame.
Subject: ZEUS from the waist up, exactly the same character as the Zeus at the top of the style reference image (silver-white swept hair, full silver beard, intense bright blue eyes, golden laurel wreath, golden shoulder plate with a blue gem on the chest strap, white draped chiton, flowing purple cape behind him, golden arm cuffs), drawn large and detailed in the same glossy casino-slot 2.5D render style. Three-quarter view, body turned slightly to the viewer's left, face looking down-left towards the slot reels below. His right arm (on the viewer's right) is bent with the hand at shoulder height beside the head and holds a crackling ice-blue and white lightning bolt pointing UP along his arm, the bolt about as long as his forearm. His left arm hangs down in front of the body on the viewer's left. Calm, powerful, a little stern expression.
Composition: portrait 2:3, the figure centred, head near the top with a small margin, the waist cut by the bottom edge of the canvas with a clean straight horizontal cut (the lower body is hidden behind the slot frame later), shoulders and both arms fully inside the canvas with a clear gap of empty background between the bolt-arm and the head and between the arm and the cape so the arm can be rotated separately later. Flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF background everywhere else.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no frame, no sky, no clouds, no floor, no shadows on the background, no glow or light halo outside the figure's own edges (the glow of the bolt stays inside the bolt's shape: crisp solid white-blue shapes with crisp edges), no motion blur. Original design only.
