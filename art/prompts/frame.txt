Use case: the ornate golden frame of a 6 columns x 5 rows slot-machine play field for a mobile ad, seen straight from the front, with an EMPTY window so the symbols can be drawn behind it.
Subject: a rich glossy polished gold frame with Greek-temple styling (laurel leaf ornaments in the corners, a small blue gemstone set in the centre of the top and the bottom edge, fine acanthus scrolls, soft bevels, bright specular highlights, warm amber shading). Above the window a wide blank polished-gold plaque plate (no text) that is part of the frame and overlaps its top edge, with small laurel wings at both ends. The window is a perfectly straight-edged rectangle with sharp square inner corners.
Composition: square 1:1 canvas, the frame fills the canvas edge to edge horizontally, symmetric left-right. The window rectangle is exactly 86% of the canvas width and 72% of the canvas height, horizontally centred, with its top edge at 22% of the canvas height, so its proportions are 6:5 (slightly wider than tall). The plaque plate sits in the top 18% of the canvas above the window; the bottom border of the frame is about 6% of the canvas height. The INSIDE of the window is flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF (it will be made transparent), and everything outside the frame (the four corners and sides beyond the frame outline) is the same flat magenta.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no digits, no symbols inside the window, no shadow on the background, no glow outside the frame, no perspective. Original design only.
